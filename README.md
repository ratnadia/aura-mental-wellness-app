🌸 Aura - AI Wellness Companion for Students

Aura is an AI-powered emotional wellness companion designed specifically for students. It provides a safe, anonymous, and judgment-free space where users can express their feelings, reflect on their emotions, and receive supportive responses powered by Google's Gemini AI.

Unlike traditional mental health applications, Aura focuses on gentle emotional support rather than clinical diagnosis, helping students feel heard and understood anytime, anywhere.

📖 Problem Statement

Many college students struggle to talk about their emotions due to:

Fear of judgment
Social stigma around mental health
Limited access to counseling services
High costs and long waiting periods for professional support

As a result, students often deal with stress, anxiety, loneliness, and emotional overwhelm without having a safe outlet to express themselves.

💡 Our Solution

Aura acts as a friendly AI companion that allows users to:

Share their thoughts anonymously
Check in with their emotions
Receive emotion-aware responses
Track mood patterns through reflections
Choose different conversation styles based on personal preference

The platform aims to provide comfort, self-awareness, and emotional support while maintaining privacy and simplicity.

✨ Key Features
🧠 Emotion-Aware Conversations

Aura detects emotions from user messages such as:

Happy
Sad
Stressed
Anxious
Overwhelmed

and adapts responses accordingly.

🎭 Multiple Companion Personalities

Users can choose how Aura interacts with them:

Calm Counselor
Supportive Mentor
Friendly Friend
Humorous Companion

This makes conversations feel more natural and personalized.

😊 Mood Check-ins

Users can quickly record how they feel through simple mood selections.

Benefits:

Easy emotional tracking
Increased self-awareness
Reflection on emotional patterns
💭 Gentle Reflection Prompts

Aura provides thoughtful prompts such as:

"I feel overwhelmed"

"I don't know how I feel"

These prompts help users start conversations even when they struggle to express their emotions.
